All IPAs are tested before being added to the repository. This may take several days. If a version is unstable or doesn't work, it won't be added.<br>

### Supported sideloaders :
**Use classic with :**
 - SideStore
 - AltStore Classic (recommended)
 - LiveContainer

## How to choose your sideloader ?

[AltStore Classic](https://altstore.io/source/marketplace.altstore.io?app=com.rileytestut.AltStore): 3 apps and 10 AppID limit without LiveContainer. You don't need a PC for installation

[SideStore](https://sidestore.io/): 2 apps and 10 AppID limit without LiveContainer. PC needed for first installation. Hard to install.<br>
